DanHuang Yan is a lovely Ragdoll cat, gentle and affectionate, always bringing warmth to those around him. known for his striking blue eyes and silky fur, and true to his breed, he loved being held like a baby.

In the summer of 2023, he passed away, leaving behind countless cherished memories and a quiet space that once echoed with his purrs.
